How claude-video turns a clip into evidence Claude can inspect

A video tutorial contains two accounts of what happened: the steps visible on screen and the explanation in the audio. The /watch tool makes both available to Claude as frames and a transcript.

1 collected itemJump to reading picks ↓ Permalink ↗

What the tool hands to Claude

In the local workflow, /watch samples timestamped frames and pairs them with captions or a transcript. If a presenter says “click here,” Claude can use a frame to identify the menu and the transcript to recover the instruction. The important design choice is when to sample: two screenshots on either side of a brief pop-up may both miss it. [1]
