When does a learned unmasking policy pay for itself?

In masked diffusion language models, choosing which tokens to reveal takes compute. A controlled study trains an 856K-parameter policy and asks whether avoiding later model evaluations earns back that choice.

Learned unmasking policy details

The study by Hugging Face introduces a learned unmasking policy for masked diffusion language models. This policy reduces the number of function evaluations (NFE) needed, achieving a 10% decrease to counteract policy overhead. The authors trained a 856K-parameter policy on trajectory-contraction targets that leverage confidence and hidden states. The results show improved throughput and accuracy on benchmarks such as GSM8K, MATH-500, and HumanEval. Additionally, the policy demonstrates effective transferability to other models, including Dream-7B-Instruct. [1]
