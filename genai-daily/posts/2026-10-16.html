Spending denoising steps where a video patch needs them

ForcingBooster changes the compute budget across patches of the same video. Gen4P uses a pretrained video generator for a different job, recovering geometry and motion without iterative denoising. Their speed claims refer to different outputs.

4D perception from video

Gen4P introduces a framework that transforms pretrained video generators into a feed-forward 4D perceiver. This method allows for the recovery of 4D geometry and motion from monocular video efficiently, utilizing on-demand querying and continuous-time queries. It incorporates a frozen geometry prior via a pretrained video-depth LoRA, which enhances performance on tasks like 3D point tracking, video depth, and camera pose estimation. Gen4P achieves state-of-the-art results, demonstrating the effectiveness of leveraging video generative pretraining for unified 4D perception. [1]

Geometry and reconstruction

Video models and tools

The paper introduces ForcingBooster, a novel framework for adaptive patch-wise denoising in autoregressive video diffusion models, which dynamically allocates denoising steps based on the complexity of individual video patches. [9]

H-DDCM introduces a scalable generative compression framework that replaces dense Gaussian codebooks with implicit randomized Hadamard dictionaries, achieving O(d log d) compute for projection and reconstruction and O(d) memory usage. [13]
