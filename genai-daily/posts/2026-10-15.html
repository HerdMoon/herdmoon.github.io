Erasing an object while the video is still arriving

StreamErase uses a sliding attention window for streaming object removal. The authors report sub-second latency and up to 26 frames per second on one GPU. For long videos, the harder test is whether an erased object stays gone after it leaves the window.

Real-time video editing

StreamErase introduces a method for real-time video object removal, achieving sub-second latency and up to 26 frames per second on a single GPU. This approach utilizes a sliding-window causal attention mechanism along with attention sinks and self-rollout techniques to ensure high visual fidelity. It also establishes the LongErase benchmark for evaluating streaming long-video object removal, demonstrating strong performance and generalization to real-world scenarios. This method significantly improves upon existing techniques that typically require minutes for video processing. [1]

World models and control

The Generative World Simulation framework integrates DAO, a computable world engine, and JING, an Egocentric Interactive Experience Model. This setup allows users to interactively explore and manipulate environments while maintaining a coherent history of actions and consequences. It enhances the realism and depth of interactive experiences, emphasizing sustained interaction for both humans and agents, which facilitates a dynamic evolution of the world state based on user actions. [2]

The K-Pop stress test evaluates monocular world-space human recovery methods under high camera dynamics. It reveals that existing methods suffer from significant drift in world-space trajectories due to scale mismatches between camera and human estimates. The authors propose a closed-form correction that aligns these scales without requiring retraining, effectively stabilizing world-space reconstruction for applications such as choreography modeling and scene editing. [4]

Video models and tools

SyncReward introduces a novel reward model for audio-visual generation, significantly enhancing synchronization between generated audio and visual events. It includes the SyncReward-Data-80K dataset with 80,000 professional synchronization ratings and the SyncReward-Bench benchmark for evaluating synchronization metrics. SyncReward achieves the highest agreement with human ratings and improves synchronization through reinforcement learning-based post-training, addressing substantial misalignment found in existing models. [3]

Glance Forcing is a lightweight distillation framework that enhances the efficiency of converting bidirectional diffusion models into autoregressive models for video synthesis. It reduces trajectory-collection costs from thousands of GPU-hours to approximately one A100-GPU-hour. The method improves the VBench Total score from 84.04 to 84.22 and achieves superior performance in dynamic degree, VisionReward, and instruction-following tasks. [5]

Multimodal learning

Apertus 1.5 extends the Apertus family from text to include image and audio understanding. This is achieved through continued pretraining on 4 trillion additional tokens for the 8B model. The context window has been expanded to 262,144 tokens, and the models demonstrate improved performance across benchmarks, particularly in knowledge and instruction following, while maintaining a text-restricted architecture. [6]

PORTAL is a training-free projector initialization method for multimodal LLMs that eliminates the need for paired image-caption data and gradient-based optimization. It leverages optimal-transport-based mapping between Gaussian approximations of vision and language feature distributions, achieving comparable performance to traditional projector training on 558k image-caption pairs while significantly reducing data dependency and training complexity. [8]

Models, methods and tools

VASPO introduces a novel approach to on-policy self-distillation by integrating privileged value estimation within the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) framework. This method enhances the accuracy of value predictions during token generation, resulting in mean accuracy improvements of 2.59 and 1.81 percentage points over standard PPO on the Qwen3-4B and OLMo3-7B models, respectively. [7]

Geometry and reconstruction

The repository "ahujasid/mcp-for-blender" introduces a community plugin that enables control of Blender 3D using any chosen large language model (LLM). This integration enhances automation and creative workflows in 3D modeling and animation, allowing users to generate commands and scripts for Blender tailored to specific needs and preferences. [9]
