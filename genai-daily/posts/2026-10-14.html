Keeping a shared 3D world consistent across viewpoints

WorldRecon reconstructs a scene mesh from RGB images without known camera poses. WorldCast tackles consistency when several players each generate their own view of a shared world. Together they make disagreement between viewpoints a concrete failure to look for.

Generative multi-view reconstruction framework

WorldRecon introduces a generative multi-view reconstruction framework that integrates a dedicated reconstruction-based conditioning architecture with the TRELLIS.2 model. This combination significantly enhances the quality of 3D scene meshes derived from unposed RGB images, achieving state-of-the-art performance on both synthetic and real-world indoor scenes. The framework employs neighbour-aware latent inpainting and a causal world-generation procedure to ensure coherent scene reconstruction across overlapping spatial blocks, addressing challenges in traditional methods. [1]

Image synthesis and editing

Dog-Breed Text-to-image Flow Matching from Pretraining to Fine-Tuning on 4 Consumer GPUs — The paper introduces MiniDog, a framework for training text-to-image diffusion models that significantly reduces compute requirements, allowing pretraining in 55 minutes and fine-tuning in 15 minutes on four NVIDIA 3090 GPUs. [2]

The paper introduces PixelMoE, a pixel-space generative framework that employs LLM-style Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures to achieve sparse scaling of model capacity in diffusion models, without the need for diffusion-specific routing mechanisms. [3]

DuplexBridge introduces a unified model that enhances image editing by coordinating understanding and generation without relying on an external understanding module. [5]

World models and control

WorldOracle introduces a benchmark for evaluating action-conditioned world models (ACWMs) against real-world executions, focusing on four dimensions: physical state correctness, interaction spatial alignment, key interaction area fidelity, and multi-view action coherence. [4]

The paper introduces UniW(A)M, a unified mixture-of-experts framework that integrates world models (WMs) and world-action models (WAMs) using diffusion Transformers, enabling shared learning of physical dynamics while maintaining distinct modeling capabilities. [7]

WorldCast introduces a distributed multiplayer world model that significantly enhances real-time performance and scalability in generating consistent views for multiple players in shared environments. [9]

GreenWAM introduces a unified world foundation model for robotics that effectively serves both world modeling and action policy roles across 20 robot families, achieving state-of-the-art performance in trajectory, depth, and 3D accuracy on the WorldArena 1.0 benchmark. [10]

The paper introduces 4LC, a 4D latent cache designed for efficient conditioning in dynamic world modeling, which enables the reuse of static and dynamic visual content for video generation without the need for per-request RGB rendering. [11]

Learning Transferable World-Action Models from Task-Paired Human Videos — The paper introduces WATCH, a novel cross-video pre-training method that enhances world-action models by learning transferable interactions from task-paired human videos across diverse environments. [12]

Video models and tools

The paper introduces Geometry Attention Distillation (GAD), a novel method that enhances video generation by improving long-range scene consistency and dynamic temporal consistency through reliable cross-frame associations. [6]

FlashVSR+ introduces a novel hybrid attention mechanism that enhances video super-resolution (VSR) by achieving up to 2x speedup and 45% lower peak memory usage, enabling efficient 4K VSR on a single 24GB RTX 4090. [13]

LightVAE introduces a novel compression framework for video autoencoders that significantly enhances decoding efficiency by reparameterizing causal Conv3D layers and applying temporal low-rank compression. [14]

Video-Arena introduces a novel benchmark for evaluating spatiotemporal understanding in video models by leveraging competitive games to create scalable tests of dynamic world state comprehension. [16]

Multimodal learning

The paper introduces OmniCompass, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating omni-modal video grounding and understanding, which includes two tracks: OmniCompass-VL for vision-language models and OmniCompass-AV for audio-visual models. [8]

Omni-Modal LLMs Know More Than They Say — The paper introduces OmniCatalyst, a novel approach that enhances the performance of omni-modal large language models (Omni-LLMs) by addressing the "knows-but-cannot-say" issue, where these models fail to effectively express non-textual information. [15]
