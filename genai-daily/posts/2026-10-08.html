Compressing video latents before the expensive generation steps

GRACE targets the number of tokens a video generator must process. JoHan also uses a generative model, but asks it to recover aligned 2D and 3D hand motion. The useful comparison for each is the detail retained after intermediate computation is removed.

Efficient video generation

The GRACE framework introduces a two-stage approach for generation-aware latent compression. By utilizing a frozen base latent from a pretrained video autoencoder, it learns a residual latent to recover lost information. This method ensures compatibility with the pretrained Diffusion Transformer (DiT) during adaptation through lightweight fine-tuning and asymmetric denoising, maintaining the generation quality of the original pipeline. The authors report an 8x reduction in token count and an 11.1x decrease in latency compared to the Wan2.1-I2V-14B model at a resolution of 480x832x81, making it a significant advancement in video generation efficiency.
