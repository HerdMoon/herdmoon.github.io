Reference Forcing improves long video generation consistency

Reference Forcing enhances long video generation by ensuring consistency in subject and background. It achieves state-of-the-art results as video length increases.

16 collected itemsJump to reading picks ↓ Permalink ↗

Video generation methods

Reference Forcing introduces a method for long video generation that uses initial frames as appearance references. It employs attention masks to ensure that new frames correspond to regions in the initial frames, achieving improved consistency as video length increases (1). [1]

MovieRoll presents a framework for long-form video generation that enhances streaming quality while reducing cache memory usage by up to 78%. It utilizes Window-wise Teacher Forcing for training and hierarchical KV-cache compression to manage historical context effectively (11). [11]

DuoFlow offers a dual-stream latent flow transformer for synchronized speech and motion generation, allowing for fine-grained control over vocal style and body movement. It enhances synchronization and instruction consistency in generated sequences (5). [5]

Manipulation and planning frameworks

Lazy-MOSAIC accelerates skill-centric manipulation planning by using a learned skill-effect world model to predict skill feasibility and outcomes quickly. This approach reduces computational burden and maintains or exceeds success rates compared to existing methods (2). [2]

LynnReal-Hand enhances interactive object manipulation through a hand-controlled video world model, achieving real-time streaming and high-quality interaction dynamics (3). [3]

JEPA4R implements 4D point planning in joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA) world models, achieving a mean point error of 1.1 cm and outperforming pixel-based models on multiple benchmarks (9). [9]
