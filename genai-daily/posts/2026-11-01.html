What should a generated world remember?

L3G-World keeps a compact 3D Gaussian representation of a scene as the viewpoint moves. Revisiting a location makes the value of that memory tangible: objects placed earlier should still occupy the same space.

Three ways to budget representation

L3G-World stores scene information in latent 3D Gaussians. A camera can leave a region and later revisit its representation. The paper's long-horizon experiments are a better place to assess that design than a short fly-through. [1]

HairDeck and SpecSlot operate at smaller scales. HairDeck treats each stylized hair card as a token, producing animation-ready cards from a single image. SpecSlot assigns distinct frequency components to slots so that preserving fine image detail need not require a longer global attention sequence. [2] [3]

Choosing a model before starting a media job

The scenario-labs/skills repository packages image, video, audio and 3D generation workflows for agents. It puts model choice and cost estimates before execution, a practical feature when the same creative request can be sent to several media services. [4]
