Giving generated video a 3D representation you can use

AEON decodes a video latent representation into renderable 4D Gaussian primitives. Joints, Camera, Action! generates video alongside motion for arbitrary rigs. Their extra outputs offer ways to inspect geometry and movement beyond watching a clip.

Unified framework for video and 3D models

AEON introduces a framework that combines video and 3D world models. It uses a generative model in a text- or image-conditioned video latent space to create renderable 4D graphics primitives via 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS). This integration improves both realism and 3D consistency in simulations, achieving state-of-the-art results on standard benchmarks. The method includes a domain adapter that translates generative video latents into reconstructive 4D latents, enabling dynamic scene rendering and persistent environment construction. [1]
