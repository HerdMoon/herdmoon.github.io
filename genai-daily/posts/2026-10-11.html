Letting a video model revise its next action

WorldGuide selects actions from the visual state it has already generated, closing the loop during a procedural task. SpaceFlow gives an artist local control over 3D geometry through text and primitives. Each offers a way to correct a generation before accepting the result.

Using feedback during generation

WorldGuide feeds generated visual states back into its planner to choose the next step of a procedure. SpaceFlow gives a human a more local kind of control: text and geometric primitives constrain different parts of a 3D generation without training a new model. In both cases, success depends on whether a requested change reaches the intended part of the output. [1] [2]

Checking geometry and correspondence

Pumpire tests whether a 3D foundation model can estimate the metric distance between two points using images or video. FreeMatching addresses the complementary problem of identifying corresponding points across views, with applications to reference-guided generation and editing. A match identifies which points to compare; a distance estimate puts a scale on them. [3] [4]

Training across tasks and fitting inference into memory

Hebero offers a GPU-parallel setting for training and evaluating reinforcement learning across 40 heterogeneous tasks. AirLLM targets inference on constrained hardware: its repository describes running a 70B language model with a single 4GB GPU. For the latter, fitting the weights and achieving useful token throughput are separate questions. [5] [6]
