Reading 3D motion out of a video generator

Gen4P repurposes a pretrained generator to recover geometry and motion from a single-camera video in a feed-forward pass. SUPRA-TEX explores the complementary single-image problem of aligning visible texture and completing surfaces outside the frame.

4D perception with Gen4P

Gen4P introduces a framework that leverages a pretrained video generator to function as a feed-forward 4D perceiver. It employs an on-demand querying mechanism for flexible spatial and temporal sampling, integrating a frozen video-depth LoRA to enhance geometric knowledge without increasing trainable parameters. This method demonstrates significant advancements in unified 4D perception capabilities, achieving state-of-the-art performance in 3D point tracking, video depth, and camera pose estimation, as reported by the authors. [1]

Geometry and reconstruction

SUPRA-TEX introduces a novel approach to single image 3D generation by decoupling visible-material estimation, surface alignment, and observation-guided 3D completion, which enhances fidelity and coherence in generated textures. [2]

Models, methods and tools

Multimodal learning

The paper introduces OmniVerifier-C1, a decomposed graded verifier that enhances the robustness of visual verifiers in complex multimodal scenarios by providing continuous pointwise scores instead of binary judgments. [4]
