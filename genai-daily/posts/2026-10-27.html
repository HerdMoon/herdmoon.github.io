An image edit you can adjust with 29 sliders

PREEN turns an example photo's color treatment into editable smartphone presets. A user can inspect and change the resulting settings. That makes preset transfer a different design problem from predicting the final pixels.

Color style transfer with PREEN

PREEN introduces a method for color style transfer that predicts a compact vector of 29 editor slider values instead of generating raster images. This approach achieves superior pixel and perceptual fidelity on the PST50 benchmark, as reported by the authors. The method employs a CLIP-conditioned regressor refined with LoRA and utilizes a style-transfer teacher to create pseudo-presets, enabling sub-second on-device latency and continuous strength adjustment for varying style intensities. This design allows users to transition smoothly between vivid and default styles without the need for retraining. [1]
