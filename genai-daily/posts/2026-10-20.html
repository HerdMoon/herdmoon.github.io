Learning robot motion from human and robot footage

Ropedia W0 brings together 10,000 hours of human experience and 3,000 hours of robot data to model locomotion and manipulation. In video production, Mise-en-Video uses a 3D layout to keep creative decisions consistent from one shot to the next.

Loco-manipulation world model

Ropedia W0 introduces an egocentric loco-manipulation world foundation model. It effectively combines locomotion and manipulation across various embodiments. The model is trained on a curated dataset, Xp-Data, which includes 10,000 hours of human data and 3,000 hours of robot data with unified multimodal annotations. This extensive training allows Ropedia W0 to achieve significant improvements in performance across five cross-embodiment benchmarks, demonstrating its effectiveness in real-time rollouts for live VR interactions and applications in policy evaluation and model-based planning. [1]
