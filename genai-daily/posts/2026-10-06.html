Changing the order of diffusion as video noise falls

S2PD applies autoregressive diffusion at high noise and switches to parallel diffusion at low noise. The division suggests a concrete experiment: vary the switch point and see which events remain physically and logically consistent.

Where video models spend attention

S2PD establishes temporal structure serially while the video is noisy, then denoises frames in parallel. Two other papers address longer rollouts. ID-Forcing examines the provenance of cached keys and values at test time. HLA-WM uses hybrid linear attention to manage the memory cost of maintaining scene consistency. [1] [12] [13]

ChronoWorld conditions 4D generation on camera control, spatiotemporal cues and geometric feedback. MC-Sparse looks inside diffusion transformers and selects individual key-value tokens for attention. These papers give different diagnostic views of a failing clip: the geometry under camera motion and the information a frame can still attend to. [4] [7]

Testing what a world model knows about actions

TAPDreamer uses a public encoder to construct transferable adversarial patches for world-action models without querying the target policy. H-JEPA studies planning at multiple timescales in an action-conditioned visual representation. A separate textual world-modeling paper tracks structured descriptions of state to limit error accumulation across a rollout. [2] [3] [8]

AffordCraft retrieves parts to build task-ready simulation assets from a single RGB image. DeskForge constructs annotated desktop scenes for computer-use supervision. Video2Skill asks whether a vision-language model can extract a reusable manipulation skill from streaming experience. Each changes the training or evaluation material a policy can learn from. [5] [9] [16]

Measuring a visual change

UniSlider remaps a generative editor's strength control so equal slider movements produce more uniform perceptual changes. DEPICT evaluates prompt-image agreement through answers to questions, including cases where a prompt explicitly excludes an object. [6] [11]

The Empirical Variational Autoencoder learns an autoregressive latent prior for continuous sequences. GeoSET uses paired synthetic-aperture radar and optical images to learn translation between sensors. OmniReasoningBench adds 1,150 questions designed to require joint use of sound and vision. [10] [14] [15]

Model releases and inference tools

DeepGEMM provides GPU matrix-multiplication kernels. Rapid-MLX serves language models on Apple Silicon and reports speed improvements relative to mlx-lm with matching weights. For reproduction, identify the exact hardware and decoding workload in each repository. [17] [18]

Two model announcements also entered the archive: Reflection's 501B open-weight Beam and Mistral Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk. Their release pages are the place to check supported modalities, memory requirements and evaluation settings before choosing a deployment target. [19] [20]
