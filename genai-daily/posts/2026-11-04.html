DreamX compresses a video transition into four action tokens

DreamX uses a pretrained video diffusion model to isolate changes that describe an action. Wonder 4 faces a different allocation problem in image restoration: it selects five of thirteen experts per block for each patch.

Latent action learning with DreamX

DreamX repurposes a pretrained video diffusion model to enhance latent action learning. It compresses video frames from 1,200 to just 4 tokens, effectively disentangling action-relevant dynamics from visual variations. This method facilitates zero-shot action transfer across different embodiments and environments, achieving state-of-the-art results on the LARY-Bench benchmark. Additionally, the shared latent action interface allows for efficient policy learning, significantly lowering the computational costs associated with video-diffusion-based policies. [1]
